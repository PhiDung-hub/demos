❯ verify lean --rules
✓passesThe kernel accepts the file, and every theorem depends only on propext, Quot.sound or Classical.choice.
✗refusedsorry, admit, custom axioms, an empty audit, a timeout or unrecognized output. Any of these leaves the proof unconfirmed.
✓sandboxRuns in a copied workspace with a private home, a 30 s limit and bounded output. It installs nothing automatically.
✓correspondenceThe repo gate pins the sha256 of every reviewed source file. If a modelled file changes, the check fails until someone reviews the proof again.

Proofs cover explicit models, and source hashes guard the link to the code. They do not prove the compiler, IO or the parsers; those get confidence scores instead.
